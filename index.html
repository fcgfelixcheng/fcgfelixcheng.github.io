About

I started with no network and no script.

2015, first year in the industry, national sales champion. I spent the decade after that working out why it happened — because a result you cannot explain is a result you cannot repeat, and certainly cannot teach.

Today FCG is a team. Ava Alicia came in 2020 and now carries RM11.3 million in GDV as a Supreme Leader. Kelvin Wong sits in the national top three. Behind them is a bench of agents who each closed their first deal with someone standing beside them, because that is how I was never taught.

What I actually do hasn't changed much: look at your numbers before your wishlist, say plainly when a project doesn't fit, and stay on the loan until the bank says yes.

How I work

Four steps, in this order.

01

Understand

Your budget, your commitments, and what the place actually has to do for you — live in, rent out, or hold. Before any viewing, not after.

02

Shortlist

Three options at most. For each one, the reason it made the list and the thing that's wrong with it. If none of the twenty fit, I'll say so.

03

Financing

Submission, follow-up, approval. This is where most purchases quietly die, so my team stays on it until the bank commits in writing.

04

Keys

SPA, progress billing, defect inspection, handover. The stretch most agents stop showing up for — and the stretch you'll remember.
