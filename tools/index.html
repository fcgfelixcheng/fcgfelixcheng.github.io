Semi-flexi and full flexi save a near-identical amount of interest; the real difference is access to your money and the small account fee.

Term loan

Fixed instalment, fixed schedule. Extra payments usually need a written request, and once paid in, the money cannot be taken back. Some banks price it slightly cheaper.

Semi-flexi Most common

Pay extra any time to cut principal and interest. Withdrawing the extra back (redraw) needs a bank request, a processing fee (commonly RM25 to 50) and a few working days.

Full flexi

Loan linked to a current account. Every ringgit parked there reduces the balance charged interest, calculated daily. Withdraw anytime. Usually a small monthly account fee (RM5 to 10), occasionally a slightly higher rate.
